Tap Bible Privacy Policy

Effective date: 26 March 2026

Overview

Tap Bible ("the Application") is a free Bible reading app for Android and iOS, created by Tap Bible. This policy explains how the Application handles your data.

Short version: the Application does not collect, store, or share any personal information about you.

Data Collection

The Application does not collect, transmit, or share any personal information. No analytics or advertising SDKs are included.

All data created while using the Application — such as bookmarks and reading preferences — is stored locally on your device and never sent anywhere.

Internet Access

The Application connects to the internet solely to download Bible translations and commentaries from the Crosswire Bible Society module repository. No personal information is included in these requests. Like any web server, Crosswire's servers may log standard request metadata (such as IP addresses) under their own policies.

Third-Party Services

The Application does not integrate any third-party services that collect data about you (such as advertising networks, analytics platforms, or social SDKs).

Children

The Application does not knowingly collect any information from children or from anyone. It is suitable for all ages.

Changes to This Policy

If the privacy practices of the Application change, this page will be updated and the effective date above will be revised. Continued use of the Application after any changes constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.

Contact

If you have any questions about this privacy policy, please contact Tap Bible at tapbible.app@gmail.com.
