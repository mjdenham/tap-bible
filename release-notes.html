Tap Bible Release Notes

What's changed in each version

v1.2.2
Oct 2026
  • Section headings now come before the verse number, and jumping to a verse shows its heading
  • The ISV and other ThML documents now show headings, footnotes and cross-references properly
  • Links to the Old Testament now work when the current Bible has only the New Testament
  • More Strong's and verse links in lexicons can be tapped
  • Commentaries such as TSK no longer show an unrelated note at the start of a book
  • Scrolling no longer sometimes opens a popup
v1.2.1
Oct 2026
  • Choosing a chapter now opens at its heading and introduction, not verse 1
  • In split screen, the other pane scrolls smoothly to a verse already on screen
  • Barnes' Notes no longer shows the previous book's last note as a chapter introduction
  • A slow download server no longer leaves the list of Bibles and commentaries stuck loading
v1.2.0
Oct 2026
  • Split a tab to read two Bibles or commentaries together
v1.1.1
Sep 2026
  • Android: read aloud now works with Samsung's speech engine
v1.1.0
Sep 2026
  • Read any Bible aloud, following along as it is read
  • A Footnotes setting to hide the * markers without Reading mode
v1.0.0
Sep 2026
  • New Spanish commentary on the whole Bible, from Matthew Henry
  • Much faster search
v0.13.0
Sep 2026
  • Fixed search failing for Chinese, Japanese, Korean, Thai and accented text
v0.12.0
Aug 2026
  • Bibles that number verses differently now stay in step — your place, bookmarks and cross-references follow you between them
  • Your reading position now survives a text-size change or a change of window width
  • Steadier verse selection — a second finger, a system-stolen touch or a small drift no longer breaks a long press
  • Strong's lookup now picks the word you pressed when a tag covers a whole phrase
  • Fixed a cold start spinning forever instead of showing "No content"
v0.11.0
Aug 2026
  • Your open documents now sit in a browser-style tab strip that scrolls, so a long name is never truncated and the tabs never run off the screen — and it gives back a strip of reading space
  • Spanish and Japanese devices now get a Matthew Henry commentary by default — New Testament to begin with, plus Psalms in Spanish
  • Verses joined together in the original text now read as one passage instead of being split
  • Fixed commentary sections appearing twice when a multi-verse entry fell across a page boundary
  • Pressing Enter to dismiss the keyboard no longer re-runs your search
v0.10.0
Jul 2026
  • Tap Bible's Matthew Henry commentaries now cover the whole Bible in Modern English and Korean, with more languages to follow — Modern English is the default commentary on a fresh English install
  • Commentaries now open at the start of the section rather than mid-passage, and scrolling a commentary no longer moves your place in the Bible
  • Update your documents straight from the Tabs screen — an open tab refreshes itself when its module is updated
  • Warmer paper-toned reading theme, with refreshed typography and a consistent look across the app
  • Fixed switching tabs sometimes landing on the wrong verse
  • Catalog search now keeps the cursor at the end of a remembered filter
  • Rebuilt the text rendering pipeline, fixing several iOS layout and continuous-scrolling defects
v0.9.0
Jul 2026
  • First Tap Bible commentaries (alpha): Matthew Henry in Modern English and Korean, browsable in the catalog — an early step toward bringing study commentary to the languages that need it most
  • Documents now update in place: tap a catalog entry to fetch the latest version, with an upgrade indicator shown when a newer version is available
v0.8.0
Jun 2026
  • Language-aware first launch: the Bible, commentary, dictionary and Strong's lexicon are chosen to match your device language
  • Redesigned first-launch screen that asks for consent before downloading in regions where that matters
  • Chinese now displays in one consistent script per region — simplified for mainland/Singapore/Malaysia, traditional for Taiwan/Hong Kong/Macau — across verses, book names, headings and menus
  • Smaller install: the bundled content is roughly halved, with the commentary and dictionary downloaded on demand; the Bible, cross-references and Strong's stay available offline
  • Updated bundled Berean Standard Bible to the 2.0 text, with bundled modules now updating automatically on startup
  • Modules now shown by their familiar abbreviation (e.g. ERV) instead of an internal name
  • More resilient first install — it retries on later launches if the first attempt was offline or interrupted
  • Fixed a white flash between the splash and download screen on first launch
  • Fixed the current-verse indicator lagging at chapter boundaries
v0.7.0
Jun 2026
  • Select multiple verses: long-press a verse, then slide to extend the selection across a range
  • Copy selected verses to the clipboard, with a confirmation toast
  • Bookmark multi-verse ranges
  • Chapter dividers now shown in commentaries
  • Theme options (Light/Dark/System) now translated in Settings
  • Loading indicator shown while switching tabs instead of "No content"
  • iOS: native back chevron and centred dialog titles
  • Improved rendering of NASB and word-order superscript markup
  • Performance improvements and dependency updates
v0.6.0
May 2026
  • Bible dictionaries: download, browse and search a range of dictionaries
  • Long-press any word in a verse to look it up across your installed dictionaries
  • Improved NASB layout
  • Fixed white flash when launching in dark mode
  • Stability and performance improvements
v0.5.0
May 2026
  • ISBE Bible encyclopedia pre-installed — tap any word for an instant definition in the popup
  • Dictionary name now shown in the word popup when multiple dictionaries are available
  • Animated splash screen on iOS
  • Tapping a link inside a popup now slides to the linked content with back navigation
  • Word highlight now appears even when Strong's numbers are not present
  • Pericope (section) titles correctly right-aligned in RTL documents
  • Improved rendering of red-letter and translator's-change markup
  • More robust word detection on iPhone SE
v0.4.1
Apr 2026
  • Polished dialog animations on iOS — entry and exit transitions now work correctly
  • Fixed back-handler registration so hardware/gesture back works in all screens
  • Migrated all dialogs and bottom sheets to a unified dialog framework
  • Fixed occasional incorrect initial scroll position on iOS when dark mode is active
v0.4.0
Mar 2026
  • Launched on the iOS App Store
  • Added translations for multiple languages
v0.3.0
Feb 2026
  • Animated splash screen on Android
  • Fixed back navigation in the book/chapter navigator
  • Search: close keyboard on submit, show "No Results" when empty, tidier history layout
  • Refreshed colour palette for better readability
v0.2.0
Jan 2026
  • Fixed TSK ThML parsing and commentary navigation for missing verses
  • Excluded duplicate eBible entries from the catalog
  • Language names now shown instead of language codes
  • More robust document downloads
  • Dark mode highlight and popup link colours improved
  • Various dialog animation and border colour fixes
v0.1.0
Dec 2025
  • Initial release on Google Play
  • Bible reading with multiple tabs and translations
  • Full-text search, bookmarks, and commentaries
  • Hundreds of languages via CrossWire and eBible catalogs
